# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Proposed / Accepted / Deprecated / Superseded by ADR-XXXX |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |
| Deciders | Names |
| Related | Requirements (FR/NFR IDs), other ADRs |

## Context

What problem or force makes this decision necessary? Include constraints.

## Options considered

1. **Option A** — pros / cons
2. **Option B** — pros / cons

## Decision

What we chose, stated in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, follow-up work, risks.
